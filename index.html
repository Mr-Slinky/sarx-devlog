Sarx

Sarx is a god game for Windows, built by hand, in Rust.

About this log

I write an entry on each day that I work on Sarx.

Engines such as Unity and Bevy already provide most of what Sarx needs, like drawing the world, running the game loop and loading models. I have chosen to build those parts myself anyway! I am aware that this is the long way round.

For now, the code is public on GitHub. Once a small demo is out, the code will move to a private repository. From then on, this log will be the public record of how the game is made.

The game

I played Black and White 2 as a kid, and fell in love with it again in my mid-20s. When I went looking for a modern successor, the best I found was Godus, and it lacked much of the character I loved from Black and White. There was no hand or creature, and the map looked like a world made of pancakes. So... I'm making my own!

In Sarx, you play as a god that reaches into the world through a large, disembodied hand. A creature lives among your followers and learns from how you treat it. Up to three rival factions share the map, and you win by defeating each one, either by siege or by building a city so impressive that its people move to yours. You know the drill...
